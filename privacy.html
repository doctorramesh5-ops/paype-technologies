Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025 · Effective date: January 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how PAYPE Technologies Private Limited ("PAYPE", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our platform at paype.co.in.

1. 📥 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, mobile number, and password when you register.
  • Payment Information: Billing details processed securely through Razorpay. We do not store card details.
  • Usage Data: Apps you create, features you use, and how you interact with our platform.
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Communication Data: Messages you send to our support team.

2. 🔄 How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To send transactional emails and SMS (OTP, receipts, alerts).
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. 🔒 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • 256-bit SSL encryption for all data transmissions.
  • Firebase Authentication for secure user management.
  • Razorpay PCI-DSS compliant payment processing.
  • Regular security audits and monitoring.

4. 🤝 Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Firebase (auth), Razorpay (payments), MSG91 (SMS), Cloudflare (security).
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or court order.
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger or acquisition (with notice to users).

5. 🍪 Cookies

We use cookies and localStorage to maintain your session, remember your preferences (theme, language), and improve your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work correctly.

6. ✅ Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information in Account Settings.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all data via Account Settings → Danger Zone.
  • Portability: Export your data by contacting us.
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails anytime.

7. 👶 Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
